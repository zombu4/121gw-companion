Review note (App Review Information, Notes):

> Free companion app for the EEVblog 121GW multimeter. It connects to the meter over Bluetooth to show live readings,
> a trend graph, recordings to CSV, the meter's SD card and firmware updates. It needs a 121GW meter to show readings;
> without one, the app opens and shows "not connected". No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected. A
> video of the app working with the meter is attached.
