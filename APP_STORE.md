# App Store submission texts

Privacy policy URL: https://github.com/zombu4/121GW-Open-Firmware/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

App Privacy (App Store Connect): Data Not Collected (voice notes: on-device speech recognition, no audio kept, nothing
sent).

Export compliance: answered in the app (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO; only the HTTPS built into iOS).

Review note (App Review Information, Notes):

> Free companion app for the EEVblog 121GW multimeter. It connects to the meter over Bluetooth to show live readings,
> a trend graph, recordings to CSV, the meter's SD card and firmware updates. It needs a 121GW meter to show readings;
> without one, the app opens and shows "not connected". No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected. A
> video of the app working with the meter is attached.

Review note, version 1.1 (added):

> Version 1.1 adds voice notes: on the Log page, switch "Voice notes" on and press Record; what the user says while
> recording is recognised on the device (SpeechAnalyzer with SpeechTranscriber on iOS 26 and newer,
> SFSpeechRecognizer with requiresOnDeviceRecognition before) and written into a
> "note" column of the CSV recording. The microphone is used only while a recording with voice notes runs; the
> "audio" background mode keeps it listening when the screen locks during such a recording. No audio is stored or
> sent.

What's new in 1.1:

> Voice notes: while you record, say what you are measuring. Your words are recognised on your iPhone and written into
> the recording next to the readings, in the row where you began speaking. No audio is kept and nothing leaves the
> phone.
