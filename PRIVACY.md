# Privacy policy: 121GW iPhone app

The app collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it
sends nothing about you or your device to anyone.

What the app does with data:

- **Bluetooth:** it talks only to your 121GW multimeter, to show its readings, record them and update its firmware.
- **Recordings and reports:** they are stored on your iPhone only. They leave it only when you share them yourself with
  the iOS share button.
- **Firmware list:** when you open the update page, the app downloads the list of firmware releases and the chosen
  firmware file from GitHub (github.com). GitHub receives the usual request, as with any web page you open.
- **Time sync:** when you sync the meter's clock, the app asks a time server (pool.ntp.org, or the one you set) for the
  current time.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/zombu4/121GW-Open-Firmware/issues
